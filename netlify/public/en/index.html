FAQ

Questions, before we start.

01How much does a project cost?

From €30/hour + VAT (€36.30 including VAT). Every project is quoted individually, based on the product, the number of images or videos and their complexity.

02How long does it take?

There are no fixed lead times: it depends on each project. Along with the quote I give you an estimated delivery date.

03Who does the work?

I do, from start to finish: modelling, materials, lighting, animation and render. You always talk to the same person.

04Who is this service for?

For brands and agencies that need a 3D render or animation of their product.

05What do you need from me to start?

Photos, drawings or measurements of the product, logos and labels, and what the images will be used for. If you can, send me the physical product: I prefer it, and it lets me reproduce materials and finishes more faithfully.

06How many rounds of changes are included?

One round of changes is included with the delivery. Extra changes are charged at €30/hour + VAT.

07What formats do you deliver?

Images as PNG or JPG and videos as MP4, in the sizes each channel needs: website, online store, social media or ads. Blender files are not included: they are delivered separately, at an extra cost, if you need them.

08Do you use AI?

Yes — as a tool, and always openly: to explore styles, generate backdrops, create variants or turn renders into video. The product always comes from the 3D model in Blender, so packaging, logo and proportions are exact. If you prefer a 100% 3D project with no AI, I do that too.
